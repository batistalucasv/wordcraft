# Validator observation contracts

Generated by `python3 crates/validate/observations/generate.py`; do not edit.
Run with `--check` to audit consumers and drift; `--self-test` exercises rejection paths.

Contracts record scope and loss policies for consumers recognized by the lexical audit; fixtures check their semantics.
The audit includes structural adapters and collection-typed locals, rejects named-field destructuring and module-scope registry aliases/queries, and checks exact declared footprints. It is not a general Rust alias or dataflow proof; Rust privacy seals the observation representation.
Only `check_custom_xml` (CUSTOMXML-SCHEMA) and `report_unreferenced_comments` (informational coverage) may consult PackageRegistryContext.
That context admits established-unowned declarations to the primary package registry; it never grants story ownership.
Duplicate relationship IDs bind the first XML declaration in every reference path; swapping duplicates changes binding evidence.
